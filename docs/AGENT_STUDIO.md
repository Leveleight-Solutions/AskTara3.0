# Agent Studio workflow and API requirements

Agent Studio helps travel agents review client information, agree a route and nights, add requested services, and produce an agency-branded proposal. The agent controls the route and final inclusions. A proposal or imported confirmation does not authorize a reservation.

The UI is available at `/studio` and `/studio/:id`. Tara collects a brief and preserves conversational route updates. The three main stages are Brief, Accommodation and Activities, with proposal preview/export available alongside them. The Daily activities tab supports manual day/activity editing, saving and reordering without an AI request, and includes the plan in proposal previews and PDF exports. Manual plans support up to 366 days; optional AI generation supports up to 35 calendar days, including transfer gaps. Dates may remain flexible. Services and proposal publication remain explicit actions. The earlier consumer itinerary planner remains a separate feature. See [implementation status](IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md) for the feature inventory and [verification results](VERIFICATION.md) for dated release evidence.

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  A[Agent notes / private source] --> B[Review brief and gaps]
  B --> C[Propose or manually edit route]
  B -->|Skip qualification| C
  C --> D[Agent accepts structure]
  D --> E[Add selected services]
  E --> F[Optional destination recommendations]
  E --> G[Preview proposal]
  F --> G
  G --> H[Create branded link / PDF]
  H --> I[Read-only client view]
```

## Services and requested recommendations

### September 24 meeting workflow

The brief includes the declared passport nationality (country only), preferred destination and country, trip length/dates, single or multiple destinations, outbound and return flight/cruise choices, and food preferences. Between-city transport remains an agent choice; the application does not optimise the route automatically.

Private client profiles use stable IDs to distinguish people with identical names. `GET/POST /api/studio/client-profiles` and `PATCH/DELETE /api/studio/client-profiles/:clientId` manage the name, context, nationality, interests, food preferences and past trips. An optional small PNG/JPEG/WebP photo is displayed to the agent, excluded from AI requests and public proposals. Passport numbers and payment/identity document information are not requested. Linking a returning client can supply travel history for research without assuming the current party is unchanged.

`POST …/destinations/research` uses a revision and requestId to research a small set of destination suggestions against current advisory and conditions evidence. It supports countries independently of the inspiration catalogue. Each candidate retains its evidence, checked timestamp and checked/warning/blocked/unknown status; missing current evidence is not a safety endorsement. Linked client history informs alternatives.

Entry checks run separately, only after the agent has selected a destination and declared the passport nationality. `POST …/entry-requirements` accepts revision, requestId and optional stopId. It compares accessible immigration and secondary index evidence for the exact pair and preserves conflicts or missing evidence as unverified. Visa-free, visa-on-arrival, e-visa and visa-required remain separate from electronic travel authorisations. This is research for agent review, not guaranteed entry. No paid visa API is added; Henley is not automatically scraped. See [travel research evidence and constraints](studio-travel-research.md).

`POST …/cruises/preview` accepts revision, requestId and an import input, returning an editable day-by-day cruise draft. `POST …/cruises/apply` accepts revision and that draft. Agents can correct any day, port, date, time or note, choose an earlier disembarkation day and record onward/return flight or cruise arrangements. Applying the edited draft preserves other manual days. Earlier disembarkation shortens the travelled plan while retaining the full supplied fare; it does not calculate a segment refund. Separate onward and return transport start as unpriced placeholders requiring agent arrangements. Reapplying a cruise preserves agent-entered transport prices, booking references and costs. A changed sailing schedule requires route and service review; it does not silently overwrite the agent’s route. Inaccessible cruise pages can be supplied as pasted text or attachments.

### Accommodation results

`POST …/hotels/search` uses the accepted stop dates, declared guest nationality and exact party. Hotel searches now support children when every age is confirmed (0–17), requesting the party in one room; flights retain their existing adults-only restrictions. Prices are requested in the brief currency and remain supplier whole-stay quotes.

Each Studio search batch requests three documented LiteAPI pages of 50 candidate hotels within 15 km of the destination centre, with up to three room offers per candidate. **Load more available hotels** requests the returned `inventory.nextOffset` (bounded to 5000) and appends results while keeping earlier quotes selectable. At the application pagination cap, `nextOffset` is null and `searchLimitReached`/`hasMore` explicitly distinguish the limit from complete supplier exhaustion. It retains every valid returned quote rather than truncating the display to twelve. The response includes `inventory` coverage and partial-failure metadata. It never claims all worldwide inventory: an empty availability page does not establish that later candidates are unavailable. Property detail requests have a bounded concurrency and time budget; unavailable descriptions, amenities and photos remain explicitly unknown.

Hotel cards show supplier photos, room names, boards, amenity/group details, price, quoted availability and expandable terms. Room photographs are shown only when the provider maps the quoted rate to that room. The optional AI shortlist selects four distinct hotels (or all when fewer than four exist) from the first batch of actual returned quotes using location, budget and availability; invalid or unavailable AI output leaves the full list accessible. Group, amenity and name/location filters operate locally without additional AI questions. Centre distance is labelled as straight-line distance and is not represented as distance to a requested neighbourhood or station.

**Add to itinerary** selects the scoped server-stored quote through `POST …/quotes/:quoteId`. It preserves the supplier amount, sandbox label, expiry and ownership checks and adds an included service without removing the manual daily plan. It does not reserve inventory or invoke any booking/payment endpoint. Reconfirm availability, cancellation policies and taxes before a separate booking.

Provider contracts: [LiteAPI rates and pagination](https://docs.liteapi.travel/reference/post_hotels-rates), [hotel details](https://docs.liteapi.travel/reference/get_data-hotel), [room mapping and photographs](https://docs.liteapi.travel/docs/room-details), and [exact adult/child occupancy](https://docs.liteapi.travel/docs/rate-request-parameters-guide). Checked on 1 October 2026; validation uses mocked providers rather than real bookings.

## Proposal pricing, publication and privacy

The proposal builder supports itemised or package pricing, separate private acquisition costs, margins and payment-cost allowances. These fields support pricing review; there is no automatic customer card fee or law-based surcharge calculator. Package mode hides component sales prices. Branding does not imply that the agency owns or operates third-party services.

Preview and publication use the following workspace routes:

- `GET …/proposal/preview` and `GET …/proposal/preview/pdf` show the private preview.
- `POST …/proposal` explicitly publishes an immutable snapshot.
- `DELETE …/proposal` revokes the published link.
- `GET /api/studio/proposals/:token` and `GET /api/studio/proposals/:token/pdf` serve the public snapshot and PDF.

Public output contains selected proposal details and excludes acquisition costs, internal margins, private sources and PNR references. Client access is read-only: commenting, acceptance, payment and email sending are not implemented. Publishing a link does not send it to anyone.

Agency price validity defaults to 48 hours and is configurable. Supplier quote age can shorten that window; republication cannot make an old supplier quote fresh. After validity ends, the page and PDF remain viewable with reconfirmation messaging. Aged prices are not presented as guaranteed current prices, and the agent can revoke a link explicitly.

## Acceptance examples

1. Enter a fictional request for Paris, Amsterdam and London in November for two adults and two children, asking for a route only. Review identifies missing exact dates and child ages; no hotel or activity generation starts.
2. Supply dates, nights and child ages or explicitly skip qualification. Reorder the stops and adjust nights. Save changes and accept the structure.
3. Paste a fictional PNR or upload a fictional screenshot. Preview and correct the text. Saving adds a private source; **Extract arrangements** creates excluded candidates for review after structure acceptance.
4. Request hotels for one accepted destination or add an externally booked service. Selecting an item includes it in the proposal without invoking a booking endpoint.
5. Request vegetarian dining suggestions for one destination. Only that category and location are enriched, with sources and no time-of-day itinerary.
6. Add an insurance estimate and package price. Preview the proposal and verify that costs, margins, source text and private booking references are absent.
7. Create a link and download the PDF. The client sees a read-only snapshot with price validity. After validity ends, reconfirmation messaging appears while the proposal remains accessible. Revoking the link removes public access.
