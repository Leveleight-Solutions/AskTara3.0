# Destination and entry research

The destination action researches a worldwide shortlist on demand. It does not claim to check every country simultaneously. Its country selector uses all 249 ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 countries/territories plus the explicitly supported Kosovo code, with Unicode CLDR names. These identifiers are not a catalogue of recommended destinations.

Each candidate requires current web-search citations for local conditions and an independently fetched FCDO advisory. The server uses the [documented GOV.UK Content API](https://content-api.publishing.service.gov.uk/reference.html) and validates the [travel-advice schema](https://docs.publishing.service.gov.uk/content-schemas/travel_advice.html). It checks the returned country and machine-readable alerts, not just AI prose. Nationwide all-travel/essential-travel warnings prevent an automated recommendation; regional, unknown, withdrawn, unavailable or unrecognised advisory data requires agent review. Missing conditions evidence published within 30 days also prevents an automated recommendation. An agent may still select a warned destination after explicitly acknowledging the warning. FCDO advice is written for British travellers; nationality-specific official advice still matters. Countries without an FCDO page, including the UK itself, remain unverified by this connector.

Entry checks run only after a destination/country and declared passport nationality exist. They target one selected route stop; a result does not cover every stop or transit country. Travel dates and declared arrival/departure modes are supplied, because cruise/port restrictions may differ. The result distinguishes visa-free, visa on arrival, e-Visa and traditional visa requirements, shows electronic authorisation separately, and retains conflicting observations. A non-unknown category requires applicable official destination-country government evidence from the current web search. Source URLs are checked against the actual search evidence. Recognised official domains include country government namespaces and relevant regional EU authorities; an unfamiliar official portal is conservatively treated as unverified. This is source corroboration, not deterministic legal verification or a guarantee of entry.

The meeting proposed Henley scraping. Its [terms of use](https://www.henleyglobal.com/terms-of-use), checked 1 October 2026, explicitly prohibit automated scraping and restrict commercial reuse without a licence. No scraper, hidden endpoint, paid visa API or access-control bypass has been added. Henley's [methodology](https://www.henleyglobal.com/passport-index/methodology) also groups visa waivers, some arrival permissions and electronic authorisations into its mobility score, and describes its information as nonbinding. A ranking alone cannot establish an individual traveller's visa category. The implemented check searches public Passport Index information when available and compares it with official immigration evidence; missing or contradictory information stays visibly unverified. No complete index dataset or licensed Henley integration is included.

Only allowlisted trip preferences, declared passport country and reduced travel-history entries reach the model. Profile photos, names, passport numbers and profile context are not sent by these research actions. Web sources and dates are retained with each result. Results older than 24 hours or affected by changed inputs are labelled for refresh. Seasonal weather guidance is labelled as such, without presenting it as a forecast for future dates.

Automated validation uses mocked Responses API output and GOV.UK content, including the current multipart response shape, active and withdrawn notices, the Russia regression, malicious source URLs, missing or wrong-country evidence, dated conditions, source disagreements, nationality mismatches and per-stop checks. Local live verification also exercised actual destination/entry research and the official Russia Content API; see [dated verification results](VERIFICATION.md). Deployment networks still need connector reachability checks. Failures remain unverified rather than being treated as favourable advice.
